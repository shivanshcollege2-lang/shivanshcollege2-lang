## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🔍 SignalGuard — Application Reliability & Incident Detection Platform *(In Progress)*
`React.js` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `Python` · `FastAPI` · `PostgreSQL`
* Telemetry monitoring platform designed to analyze API latency, error rates, traffic patterns, and application logs to identify abnormal service behavior and accelerate incident investigation.
* **Key areas:** Real-time telemetry ingestion, statistical anomaly detection, automated risk scoring, and service-health dashboards.

---

### 🧾 InvoiceEye — Smart Invoice Verification System
`React.js` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MySQL` · `Python` · `Tesseract OCR` · `JWT`
* Full-stack document processing and invoice verification system built to automate first-level supplier invoice checks prior to payment approval.
* **Key areas:** Multi-format OCR intake pipeline (Python + Tesseract OCR), deterministic rule-based validation (line-item arithmetic, PO matching, duplicate flagging), explainable audit trails, and role-based Company/Vendor portals.

---

### ⚡ ChargeFlow — EV Charging Station Management & Booking Network
`React.js` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MySQL` · `JWT`
* Three-tier EV charging platform simplifying station discovery, charger filtering, conflict-free time-slot reservations, and live charging-session lifecycle tracking.
* **Key areas:** Server-side interval-overlap booking engine, decoupled session/billing tracking (kWh & cost), and JWT-protected operator dashboards.

---

### 🔐 CampusPass — Anti-Proxy QR Gate Security & Smart Attendance Portal
`React.js` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MySQL` · `Google OAuth 2.0` · `JWT` · `Firebase`
* Role-based campus access-management and attendance portal engineered to digitize student entry/exit passes and eliminate proxy entries.
* **Key areas:** Single-use anti-proxy QR engine (auto-invalidates on first scan), live camera identity verification, automated daily classroom attendance ledger, and Google OAuth 2.0 + JWT RBAC across Student, Security, Teacher, and Admin roles.

---

### 📊 DataPilot — CSV Analytical Dashboard
`Python` · `Pandas` · `Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)` · `Data Preprocessing` · `Data Visualization`
* End-to-end data analytics application designed to transform raw tabular CSV datasets into structured, interactive, business-oriented insights.
* **Key areas:** Automated CSV ingestion, data profiling and cleaning, KPI calculation (revenue, profit, average order value), trend/category visualizations, and transaction-level search.

---

### ✍️ BlogSphere — Full-Stack Editorial Blogging Platform
`React.js` · `Node.js` · `Express.js` · `MongoDB` · `Mongoose` · `JWT` · `Vite` · `Zod` · `bcryptjs`
* Full-stack publishing platform connecting readers and writers through an editorial-style experience with secure session management and personalized author dashboards.
* **Key areas:** User authentication, full CRUD article management, category-based filtering, topic search, schema validation with Zod, and responsive UI.
